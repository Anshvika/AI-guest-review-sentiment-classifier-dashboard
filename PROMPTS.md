# Week 7 AI Prompt Log

## Project
AI Guest Review Sentiment Classifier Dashboard

---

## Prompt 1

### Input
Give recommendations to improve customer satisfaction.

### Output
The AI analyzed the guest reviews and suggested improvements such as:
- Improve cleanliness and hygiene.
- Reduce noise disturbances.
- Improve food quality.
- Fix bathroom facilities and water pressure.
- Continue maintaining excellent hospitality and customer service.

---

## Prompt 2

### Input
Summarize all guest reviews.

### Output
The AI summarized the overall guest feedback by identifying positive experiences, negative experiences, and the overall sentiment across all reviews. It highlighted common praises such as friendly staff, scenic locations, and cleanliness, while also mentioning recurring complaints including hygiene, noise, food quality, and bathroom issues.

---

## Prompt 3

### Input
Show the overall review statistics.

### Output
The AI generated review statistics from the database, including:
- Total number of reviews
- Number of Positive reviews
- Number of Neutral reviews
- Number of Negative reviews
- Overall sentiment distribution based on the available guest reviews.

---

## Best Prompt

**Prompt:** Show the overall review statistics.

**Reason:**
This prompt produced the most informative response because it provided a clear summary of the review dataset, including sentiment distribution and overall review counts. It offers valuable insights for hotel owners to quickly understand guest feedback.

---

## System Prompt Used

The AI assistant was instructed to:

- Analyze guest reviews stored in the MongoDB database.
- Use only the available review data.
- Summarize guest feedback accurately.
- Generate recommendations based on review trends.
- Display review statistics when requested.
- Avoid generating information that is not present in the stored reviews.
